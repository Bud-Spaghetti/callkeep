How CallKeep works

A custom workflow that answers when your shop can't, collects caller contact info and what service they need, and gets that to you—so jobs don't go to the next Google listing.

1

We map how your shop runs

Short conversation with you (or your office lead): services you take, areas you cover from Castle Rock through Denver metro suburbs, after-hours expectations, and how you prefer to receive leads. No one-size-fits-all script dump—we align the workflow to your business.

2

Calls get handled when you can't

When you're closed, on a job, or the line is busy, CallKeep engages so the caller isn't stuck at voicemail. We collect who they are, how to reach them, and what service they need—in clear terms you can act on.

3

Leads land with you

You get the contact details and job request promptly. You decide whether to call back immediately, schedule for morning, or send a tech. Quoting, pricing, and dispatch stay yours.

Built for after-hours answering service needs—without a call center payroll

Many contractors and shops in Colorado look for 24/7 call answering for local service businesses but don't want the cost or complexity of a traditional answering service. CallKeep is aimed at that gap: capture after-hours calls for your service business, keep the customer from dialing the next listing, and put a usable lead in your hands.

What we gather from each caller

  • Name and best callback number
  • What service they need (and urgency, when they share it)
  • Location context so you can confirm it's in your coverage area

What stays with your shop

  • Final say on which jobs you take
  • Pricing, estimates, and scheduling
  • Customer relationships and follow-up
