Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about after-hours answering and client capture for Colorado contractors, shops, and local service businesses.

What is CallKeep?

CallKeep is a custom workflow for local service businesses that answers when the shop can't, collects caller contact info and what service they need, and gets that to the shop so jobs don't go to the next Google listing.

Is CallKeep an HVAC answering service for Denver and Colorado?

Yes. We work with HVAC contractors in Castle Rock, south Denver, and Denver metro who need after-hours and busy-line coverage without standing up a full call center. More on our HVAC page.

Do you offer after-hours answering service for plumbers?

Yes. Plumbing shops use CallKeep to capture emergency and after-hours callers when the office is closed or every plumber is already on a job. See plumbing.

Is this 24/7 call answering for contractors in Castle Rock and Denver metro?

CallKeep is built to cover times your shop can't answer—nights, weekends, and busy periods. Exact coverage is set during setup for your trade and hours. Service area details.

How do I capture after-hours calls for my service business?

Reach out to set up a workflow that engages when you can't pick up, gathers contact info and service need, and delivers the lead to you. Start on the contact page, call 303-915-3605, or email corywilliam3@gmail.com.

Who is an answering service for local service businesses in Colorado for?

CallKeep serves contractors and shops, trades and mobile services that compete locally and lose work when calls go unanswered. HVAC, plumbing, and electrical are examples—not the only verticals. See all industries, including electrical, towing, and auto repair.

Do you work with towing companies and auto repair shops?

Yes. Towing companies and auto repair / auto shops are welcome. After-hours and busy-line demand is a daily reality in those businesses—CallKeep captures the caller contact info and what they need when you can't pick up. More on our towing and auto repair pages.

Do you take over quoting and dispatch?

No. We capture and deliver the lead. Pricing, scheduling, and dispatch stay with your shop. How it works.

Where is CallKeep based?

Castle Rock, Colorado area. We work with shops across Castle Rock, Parker, Highlands Ranch, and Denver metro suburbs. About CallKeep.
