About CallKeep

CallKeep exists for one reason: local service shops lose real jobs when the phone isn't answered after hours or during busy days.

What we do

We set up a custom workflow that answers when the shop can't, collects caller contact info and what service they need, and gets that to the shop—so jobs don't go to the next Google listing. We work with contractors and shops, trades and mobile services—including HVAC, plumbing, electrical, towing, auto repair, and similar after-hours demand businesses—in Castle Rock, south Denver, and the broader Denver metro.
