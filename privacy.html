Privacy Policy

Short stub—last updated September 19, 2026. This page will be expanded as CallKeep grows.

Who we are

CallKeep is operated by Cory William Ernest (William) in the Castle Rock, Colorado area. Contact: corywilliam3@gmail.com, 303-915-3605.

Information we collect

When you call or email us, we receive the information you choose to share (name, business name, phone, email, and details about your shop's needs). This marketing website does not use heavy analytics scripts or advertising trackers at this time.

How we use information

We use inquiry details to respond to setup requests and to provide CallKeep services if we work together. We do not sell your contact information.

Caller data from client workflows

When CallKeep captures leads for a client shop, caller contact info and service needs are collected to deliver those leads to that shop. Handling of that data is governed by the working agreement with the client business. Homeowners with questions about a specific contractor's practices should contact that contractor directly.

Third parties & hosting

This static site is prepared for hosting on Cloudflare Pages (or similar). Hosting providers may process standard server logs (IP address, user agent, request time) as part of delivering the site.
