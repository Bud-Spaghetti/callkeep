## Week 2 — Local Colorado / Douglas County presence

- [ ] **Castle Rock / Douglas County Chamber of Commerce**  
  - Membership + directory listing if budget allows. Strong local trust signal for B2B.

- [ ] **Town / city resource lists**  
  - Check Castle Rock Chamber partner directories, Douglas County small business resources, and any “local tech / professional services” lists—only claim accurate categories.

- [ ] **Colorado small business directories (selective)**  
  - Colorado Small Business Development Center (SBDC) events/networking → relationships first, links second.  
  - Skip paid “guaranteed ranking” directory packages.

- [ ] **Industry associations (owner networking)**  
  - Attend or join circles where HVAC / plumbing / electrical owners already gather (local trade meetups, supplier open houses). Ask for a website mention only after you’ve helped someone.

## Week 3 — Partner & content-adjacent links (earned)

- [ ] **Supplier / partner mentions**  
  - HVAC supply houses, plumbing wholesalers, electrical distributors, towing/auto parts partners in south Denver: offer a short one-pager for their counter staff (“what we tell shops that miss after-hours calls”). A link from *their* blog or resources page beats 50 spam directories.

- [ ] **Guest posts later (not day one)**  
  - Pitch useful articles to **partner trade / shop blogs** or local business blogs, e.g.:  
    - “What happens to after-hours calls when your trucks are all out”  
    - “How Castle Rock local service businesses lose jobs between 6pm and 8am”  
    - “Roadside demand after midnight: why towing companies miss jobs on a busy line”  
  - One good guest post with a contextual link to `/how-it-works.html` or an industry page > ten thin “article marketing” sites.

- [ ] **Local podcasts / YouTube interviews**  
  - South Denver small-business or contractor podcasts: interview = authority + often a show-notes backlink.

- [ ] **Angi / HomeAdvisor / Thumbtack — careful**  
  - These platforms skew **homeowner** lead-gen, not B2B.  
  - Only create a profile if you have a clear B2B positioning and won’t get buried as a consumer “answering service” confusion. Prefer skipping until messaging is proven.

## Explicitly avoid

- Private Blog Networks (PBNs)  
- Mass automated directory submissions  
- Buying backlinks or “DA50 guest posts”  
- Fake reviews / fabricated case studies  
- Exact-match spam domains linking to the site  
- Cloaking or doorway pages for homeowner emergency keywords (this site is **B2B**)

## Success signals after 30 days

- GBP (if launched) verified and complete  
- Search Console receiving crawl data; sitemap processed  
- 5–15 clean citations with matching NAP  
- 1–3 earned mentions (Chamber, partner, or guest content) in progress  
- Conversations with real shop owners—not vanity link metrics  

When the custom domain goes live, update every citation URL in the same week to avoid split signals.
