Towing is one of the industries we help. Roadside demand does not wait for a free line. CallKeep helps towing companies capture after-hours and busy-dispatch calls before they hit the next listing.

One of the industries we help: towing companies

Breakdowns, lockouts, and accident tows stack up nights and weekends—and during the day when every truck is already out. If dispatch can't pick up, the caller dials the next tow company on Google. CallKeep answers when the shop can't, collects caller contact info and what they need (tow, jump, lockout, recovery), and gets that lead to you.

Core offer: a custom workflow so jobs don't go to the next Google listing—especially useful if you've been searching for towing answering service Denver or after-hours call capture for towing without wanting a full call-center contract.

What towing operators typically want covered

  • After-hours and overnight roadside requests
  • Overflow when every truck is on a call
  • Clear callback details—location context and service need—so dispatch isn't guessing
